VIEC #285 - Lam bai tap de hon (LB-Q1..Q4)

CHI BAM 1 TEP: 1-cap-nhat-web.bat

Thu tren dien thoai sau khi web cap nhat:
  - Trac nghiem / Nghe noi: tra loi dung -> nut "Cau tiep 3..2..1" tu sang; bam la sang ngay. Cong tac "Tu sang cau: Bat/Tat".
  - True or false: chi bam True / False (bo o Noi/Go); dung thi tu sang sau 3 giay, sai thi dung lai.
  - Nghe noi / True or false tren dien thoai mac dinh "Ca hai" (hien chu), khong con khung vang.
  - Trong nhom: dieu kien chi con 1 dong nho; dat roi moi hien thanh "Da xong phan nay" o day man.
  - Det thanh nut tron nho tren thanh dau khi lam bai tap; keo di cho khac duoc; dau x de tat.
  - Khung "xem huong dan?" o dau man, khong che dap an.
  - Truong nhom: nut "Chep loi nhac gui Zalo"; dai "Bai X vua mo - Chep thong bao"; "Gioc ban" chep san cau kem link.
  - Nhom moi: Nghe noi mac dinh 30/40 (nhom cu giu nguyen).
