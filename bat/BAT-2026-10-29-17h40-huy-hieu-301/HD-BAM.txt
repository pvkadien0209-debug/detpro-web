VIEC #301 - Huy hieu nhom hoc

CHI BAM 1 TEP: 1-cap-nhat-web.bat  (gom ca #285, #290, #291, #298, #300)

Det trao huy hieu ngay khi dat:
  - Ve dich dau tien Bai X (nguoi dau tien xong ca 5 phan bai do)
  - Chuoi 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 / 60 / 100 ngay
  - Ve dich ca chuoi
Khi trao: tin chuc mung trong Hoi dap + dong viec co nut Khen.
Cuoi moi chang: tin "Ca nhom xong Bai X" kem dong "Huy hieu chang nay: ...".
Xem: bam ten mot nguoi tren cay -> hang huy hieu tren cay cua nguoi do; nut ten co so huy hieu.
