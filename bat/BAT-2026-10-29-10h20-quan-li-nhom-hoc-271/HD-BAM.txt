  1-dat-mat-khau-quan-li.bat : go mat khau quan ly (an chu). Luu vao .env.local tren may.

  2-cap-nhat-web.bat         : giai nen + day len web (gom ca #268 #269 #270).

Dung: mo https://detpro.vercel.app/quanlinhomhoc -> go mat khau.
  - Danh sach moi nhom (tim theo ma, ten, chuoi, truong nhom), bam mot nhom de xem chi tiet.
  - Sua: ten nhom, truong nhom, dieu kien qua phan, thoi han, nhac, kenh Zalo, so nguoi, khoa nguoi moi, tat Hoi dap.
  - Thanh vien: doi ten, Dang hoc / Tam nghi, Them PIN / Dat PIN moi, Mo khoa (go sai PIN), xem Nhat ky.
  - KHONG xoa nhom, khong xoa nguoi, khong bo PIN.
  - PIN cu khong xem duoc (ma hoa mot chieu) - quen thi dat PIN moi roi bao nguoi do.
  - Trang khong co link nao tro toi, Google khong lap chi muc. Sai mat khau 5 lan -> cho 1 phut.
