Building an EPC contract backwards

Start from what each side needs on the last day, then ask: to get that, what must already exist? Walk back milestone by milestone, from Final Completion to Signing, until every need lands on an Article of the reference EPC contract (DT1).

    Practice: spot what is missing

    Three kinds of exercise for each milestone. Detail (50): one clause with one to three protections removed or weakened; find what the clause lacks. Overview (50): the milestone's whole group of clauses, unnamed and shuffled, with one or two mechanisms missing entirely and sometimes a detail gap inside one clause; find what the package lacks. Contents: every bold line one side needs at one milestone (for example all of B1 and B2 for the Contractor at Final Completion), shown without codes, with one to three lines taken out (the number is not shown). Answer five steps: which milestone, whose stance (Employer or Contractor), what that side mainly wants at this milestone, which lines are missing, and what that side wants from them, using that side's own set of stance tags (E- for the Employer, C- for the Contractor). Each milestone has its own set, and the button below draws 30 at random from the whole tree. Read the lesson first (the milestone's nodes in the tree), then practise. The order is reshuffled every time you open a set. Answers can be one tag or several, and the number is hidden unless you turn on the hint.
